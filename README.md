## Liz Orembo

**Tech policy and communications · AI governance · data governance · information integrity**

I lead International and Regional Engagement at [Research ICT Africa](https://researchictafrica.net/author/liz-orembo/), where my work traces how structural and institutional conditions determine whether Africa's digital transformation ambitions translate into meaningful outcomes. I work across research, policy processes and public communication: I write the analysis, convene the people who need to act on it, and explain the technical detail in terms decision-makers can use.

### Where my work sits
- **Kenya:** Board member, [KICTANet](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/) · Chair, Africa Tech Policy Summit · Chair, Kenya Internet Governance Forum Multistakeholder Advisory Committee · Member of the Technical Committee developing Kenya's national AI policy
- **Africa and global:** Convened African stakeholders on AI governance, WSIS+20 and the Global Digital Compact · Engages the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance and UN cyber capacity processes · Freedom Online Coalition Advisory Network · Heinrich Böll Foundation Global Majority AI Fellow, 2026

### What I bring
- **Policy research and analysis:** AI governance, data governance for the public good, platform regulation, information integrity and elections
- **Strategic communications:** led communications strategy for the University of Oxford's Global Cybersecurity Capacity Centre across partners in the UK, Africa and Australia; policy briefs, consultation submissions, convenings, talks and moderation
- **Technical grounding:** trained journalists and activists in digital security; worked with African governments on national cybersecurity capacity assessments; Internet governance trainer with the African Union; contributed to Kenya's UNESCO Internet Universality (ROAM-X) indicators assessment
